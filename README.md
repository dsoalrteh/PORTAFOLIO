# Data Analyst Human Resources
PORTAFOLIO DATA ANALYST

Data Used
Data - HR Data with over 22000 rows from the year 2000 to 2020.

Data Cleaning and Analysis - SQL Server

Data Visualization - PowerBI

Summary of Findings

1. Gender Distribution: The workforce consists of a higher proportion of male employees, although the gender distribution across departments remains relatively balanced.

2. Ethnic Representation: The majority of employees identify as White, while Native Hawaiian and American Indian individuals represent the smallest demographic groups.

3. Age Range: Employee ages range from 20 to 57 years.

4. Age Group Breakdown: Employees were categorized into five age groups (18–25, 26–35, 36–45, 46–55, and over 56). The largest age group is 46-55, followed by 36-45. The smallest representation is in the 18-25 age group.

5. Work Location: A significant majority of employees are based at the company’s headquarters, with fewer working remotely.

6. Average Tenure of Terminated Employees: Terminated employees had an average employment duration of approximately seven years.

7. Turnover by Department: The Marketing department reports the highest turnover rate, followed by the Training department. The lowest turnover rates are observed in the Research & Development, Support, and Legal departments.

8. Geographic Distribution: A considerable number of employees are based in the state of Ohio.

9. Departmental Tenure: The average tenure across departments is approximately eight years. The Legal and Auditing departments have the longest average tenure, while the Services, Sales, and Marketing departments have the shortest.
